Limits: name 30, subtitle 30, promotional text 170, keywords 100
characters (comma-separated, no spaces needed).

## English (U.S.), primary

- **Name**: PickleBall: Score & Belts
- **Subtitle**: Score pickleball and padel
- **Promotional text**: New: brackets, pools and Mexicano nights, a level for each sport, and your season recap. Come for the belt.
- **Keywords**: pickleball,padel,score,scorekeeper,tournament,bracket,americano,mexicano,watch,league,ladder,rating
- **Description**:

  Score pickleball and padel on your Apple Watch or iPhone. Your friends confirm every result, and the belt goes to whoever beats the holder.

  PLAY
  • One tap per rally on your wrist or phone. The app knows serves, side-outs, deuce and tiebreaks.
  • Forgot to score? Type it in afterwards.
  • Heart-rate zones and calories from Apple Watch.

  COMPETE
  • Belts for every rivalry and every squad, with gold and undisputed tiers.
  • A personal level for each sport, worked out from confirmed results.
  • Squad ladder: beat someone above you and take their rung.
  • Tournaments from your phone: round robin, King of the Court, Americano, Mexicano, knockout, double elimination, and pools into a knockout.

  WATCH AND SHARE
  • Share a live scoreboard anyone can open in a browser.
  • Your season recap: matches, belts, your nemesis and your best partner.

  FRIENDS ONLY
  • Chats for every friend and squad, call outs, and Serves only your friends see.
  • Nothing is public unless you share a live page.
  • Report and block everywhere; a real person reviews reports.

  Low-key by design: a small belt widget and at most one short nudge a day.

- **What's New** (first version): First release. Come for the belt.

## App Privacy (privacy labels)

- **Do you or your third-party partners collect data?** Yes.
- **Tracking**: No, nothing is used for tracking.

| Data type | Linked to the user | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Contact info → Name | Yes | App Functionality |
| Identifiers → User ID | Yes | App Functionality |
| Identifiers → Device ID (random install ID) | No | Analytics |
| User content → Photos or Videos | Yes | App Functionality |
| User content → Emails or Text Messages (in-app chat) | Yes | App Functionality |
| User content → Other User Content (posts, match results) | Yes | App Functionality |
| Health & Fitness → Health (heart rate) | Yes | App Functionality |
| Health & Fitness → Fitness (workouts) | Yes | App Functionality |
| Location → Coarse Location (court you tag) | Yes | App Functionality |
| Usage data → Product Interaction (daily app open) | No | Analytics |
| Diagnostics → Crash Data | No | Analytics, App Functionality |
| Diagnostics → Performance Data | No | Analytics, App Functionality |

Not collected: email (only if the user shares it through Sign in with
Apple; it isn't stored in the profile), precise location, contacts,
browsing or search history, purchases, financial info, sensitive info.

Contacts: "Invite from contacts" reads names and phone numbers on the
device, after the user allows it, only to open Messages with an invite.
Nothing is uploaded, so Contacts stays "not collected".

These answers match `PickleBall/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.

## Age rating

Answer **None** to every content question (violence, sexual content,
profanity, drugs, gambling, horror and so on), then:

- **Unrestricted web access**: No
- **User-generated content**: Yes. The app offers reporting, blocking and a
  moderation queue reviewed by a person.
- **Messaging and chat**: Yes (with friends only)
- **Gambling / contests**: No

Expected result: **13+** (Apple's current tiers). The terms ask for 14+ in
Spain and Italy, which is stricter than the store rating, and that's fine.

## App Review Information

- **Sign-in required**: Yes. Provide two demo accounts, **A** and **B**,
  already friends and in one squad, with a few confirmed matches.
- **Notes**:

  > PickleBall is a friends-only scorekeeper. Results need the other side to
  > confirm them: sign in as A, enter a score against B, then sign in as B
  > (on a second device, or sign out and back in) and confirm it under Play.
  > Moderation: Me → Settings → Moderation is visible to admin accounts only;
  > account A is an admin. Account deletion: Me → Settings → Delete account.
  > The Apple Watch app scores matches; it works without the phone nearby
  > once a match starts.
